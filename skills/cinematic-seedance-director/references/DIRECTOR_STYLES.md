# Director & Cinematographer Style Library

> The authoritative reference for matching script tone/emotion to the world's greatest visual storytellers.
> Use this file to intelligently select a cinematographic style for each scene, or to honor a user-specified director/DP reference.

## 1. Roger Deakins — Naturalistic Master

**Films:** Blade Runner 2049, 1917, No Country for Old Men, Sicario, Skyfall

### Core Philosophy
- Light is always motivated by a visible or implied source within the scene
- Shadows are deep, deliberate, sculptural — never flat black
- Prefers hard directional light over soft fill
- Color palette is controlled and desaturated; one accent color per scene
- Camera moves only when emotion demands movement; otherwise holds still

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Wide compositions with strong negative space; subjects often placed at frame edge (1/3 line) rather than center
- **Depth:** Deep focus for environmental storytelling; shallow focus only for intimate emotional close-ups
- **Movement:** Slow push-ins (1-2mm/sec feel), imperceptible pans, smooth tracking on Steadicam or dolly. Never handheld unless character panic demands it
- **Lens preference:** 35mm (environmental), 50mm (dialogue), 75mm or 100mm (close emotion). Avoids extreme wide or extreme long lenses

### Composition
- Architecture and geometry frame the subject: doorways, corridors, windows create natural frames-within-frames
- Horizon lines are deliberate — high horizon for ground-level vulnerability, low horizon for sky/environmental power
- Silhouettes against motivated light sources (windows, door openings, screens, fires)

### Rhythm
- Long takes with patient holds; cuts only when emotional beat demands
- Silence and negative space carry as much weight as action
- Camera tempo matches character breathing — slow during contemplation, accelerating during threat

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
自然光源驱动：[窗户/门缝/灯泡/屏幕/火光]投射的方向性硬光，阴影深邃富有雕塑感，画面保持[冷青/暖琥珀/银白]调性，仅保留一抹[强调色]点缀。拒绝补光灯、反光板。从暗侧拍摄。
```

### Best For
- Cold war thrillers, neo-noir, survival dramas, existential sci-fi, contemporary westerns

## 2. Emmanuel Lubezki — Long-Take Naturalist

**Films:** The Revenant, Birdman, Children of Men, Gravity, The Tree of Life

### Core Philosophy
- Natural light only — shoots during golden hour, blue hour, overcast days; never artificial fill
- Long takes (3-15 min) where the camera becomes a participant, not an observer
- 65mm large format for organic texture and enormous depth of field
- Wide-angle lenses (24-32mm) kept close to faces for intimacy without distortion
- Camera orbits the character in 180-360 degree arcs; eyeline breaks intentionally

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Extremely wide establishing shots → rapid push to extreme close-up in same take
- **Movement:** Continuous flowing camera — Steadicam orbits, handheld tracking, vehicle-mounted. Movement is organic, never mechanical
- **Lens preference:** 24mm, 28mm, 35mm. Large format (65mm) for fine-grain detail. Rarely exceeds 50mm

### Composition
- Characters dwarfed by nature/environment in extreme wide shots
- Over-the-shoulder compositions are intimate and physical — camera nearly touching the subject
- Foreground elements (leaves, fabric, glass, steam) create layered depth
- Horizon is unstable — Dutch angles and organic tilts during moments of disorientation

### Rhythm
- Real-time pacing: scenes unfold at the speed of human experience
- Silence, ambient sound, and breath dominate sound design
- Action sequences use long takes instead of coverage + cuts

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
纯自然光拍摄：[黄金时刻/蓝色时刻/阴天/月光]均匀漫射，65mm大画幅质感，皮肤纹理细腻可见。镜头以流畅手持/稳定器环绕角色，从[方向]接近至极特写。前景[叶片/织物/蒸汽]制造景深层次。无任何人造补光。
```

### Best For
- Survival epics, immersive war, oneiric/dreamlike drama, spiritual journeys, natural-world stories

## 3. David Fincher — Precision Engineer

**Films:** Se7en, Fight Club, Zodiac, The Social Network, Gone Girl, Mindhunter

### Core Philosophy
- Camera is a machine: every movement is reproducible, robotic, unnervingly precise
- Dark, deep shadows with controlled pools of warm light
- Symmetrical or grid-based framing — oppressive geometric environments
- Locked-off camera for dialogue; smooth controlled movement only for reveals
- Desaturated palette with sickly green/amber undertones

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Clinical symmetry, centered subjects, grid-aligned architecture
- **Movement:** Computer-controlled pan/tilt/dolly — imperceptibly slow, disturbingly smooth. Never handheld
- **Lens preference:** 27mm, 35mm, 40mm anamorphic. Consistent lens set across entire film

### Composition
- Symmetrical framing centered on architectural axis
- Negative space behind subject creates isolation/unease
- Deep focus everywhere — everything is in focus, nothing escapes notice
- Top-down flat framings (tables, documents) as insert punctuation

### Rhythm
- Tight, controlled editing — average shot length 2-4 seconds
- Slow burn: deliberate pacing that compresses into sudden violence
- Camera tempo is metronomic — unchanging speed regardless of on-screen action

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
受控暗调：深暗阴影中仅保留[暖琥珀/冷绿]色光池，对称构图中角色居中。镜头运动机械精准、极度流畅。消饱和调色保留轻微[绿色/琥珀]底色。一切景深清晰可见。
```

### Best For
- Psychological thrillers, crime procedurals, tech/digital-age dramas, dark social commentary

## 4. Robert Richardson — Angular Provocateur

**Films:** Kill Bill, Django Unchained, The Hateful Eight, JFK, Hugo

### Core Philosophy
- High-contrast, saturated, visually aggressive
- Low-angle compositions that empower subjects and architecture
- Hard key light with deep, rich blacks
- Anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen for mythic scale
- Warm, golden skin tones against cool or black environments

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Extreme low angles (camera near floor), radical Dutch angles for tension
- **Movement:** Dynamic whip pans, crash zooms, aggressive push-ins
- **Lens preference:** 40mm anamorphic for faces, 75mm for isolated beauty shots

### Composition
- Subject dominates the frame, often centered or pushed to edges with dynamic framing
- Ceilings visible in interior shots (from low angle) — creates claustrophobic power
- Bright practical light sources (lamps, fires, neon) blow out into anamorphic flares

### Rhythm
- Editing is musical — cuts on beats, rhythm drives the sequence
- Slower scenes use long holds; action uses rapid, aggressive cutting
- Sound design is loud and tactile — every hit, breath, footstep is amplified

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
高反差激进：[钨丝灯/霓虹/火光]硬光直射，皮肤暖金，环境冷暗。极低角度仰拍，变形宽银幕2.39:1。光晕和眩光保留。画面饱和度高。
```

### Best For
- Tarantino-style violence, revisionist westerns, 1970s homages, rock-and-roll energy

## 5. Christopher Doyle / Wong Kar-wai — Chromatic Intimacy

**Films:** In the Mood for Love, Chunking Express, 2046, Happy Together

### Core Philosophy
- Color is emotion: saturated reds, teals, greens, golds wash every frame
- Time is distorted — step-printing (slow shutter), motion blur, doubled exposures
- Camera is handheld, intimate, searching — never clinical
- Natural/practical light sources pushed to extreme color (green fluorescent, red neon, golden sodium vapor)
- Frame rates manipulated: 8-12fps for dreamlike motion, then printed at 24fps

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Close, claustrophobic — faces pressed to frame edge, reflections in mirrors/glass/water
- **Movement:** Breath handheld — drifting, swaying, orbiting slowly; never still
- **Lens preference:** 35mm, 50mm at wide aperture (F1.2-F1.8) for shallow DOF

### Composition
- Doorways, windows, curtains, railings partially occlude the subject — we spy on intimacy
- Color-drenched environments: red walls, green-tinted glass, neon signage reflected in rain
- Space is ambiguous — we rarely see full rooms; fragments and reflections compose the scene

### Rhythm
- Elongated moments: a glance held for 8 seconds, a walk down a hallway stretched to 30
- Step-printed slow motion creates dreamlike temporal displacement
- Music-driven: entire sequences cut to a single song

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
色彩即情感：[荧光灯绿/霓虹红/钠灯金]饱和浸染画面，手持呼吸感镜头，浅景深。角色被[门框/窗帘/玻璃]部分遮挡。步印缓速（降帧拍摄后24fps放映），运动模糊如梦。
```

### Best For
- Urban romance, melancholic longing, night-city stories, Asian New Wave, time/memory themes

## 6. Hoyte van Hoytema — Spatial Minimalist

**Films:** Tenet, Dunkirk, Interstellar, Oppenheimer, Spectre

### Core Philosophy
- Large format (65mm/IMAX) for immense resolution and organic grain
- Horizontal breadth and scale — environments dwarf human figures
- Available/natural light sources pushed to maximum; minimal supplementation
- Practical locations over sets; real textures (concrete, steel, fabric, skin)
- Camera is often still or moving on a single axis — orthogonal, architectural

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Extreme wide with tiny subjects; medium shots feel architectural
- **Movement:** Smooth orthogonal dolly tracks, crane descents/ascents, helicopter-scale aerials
- **Lens preference:** 28mm, 35mm, 50mm on large format (equivalent to 18mm, 24mm, 35mm on 35mm)

### Composition
- Horizon lines dominate — sky occupies 2/3 of frame in landscape shots
- Human figures are specks in vast environments
- Architectural geometry creates leading lines and natural frames
- IMAX 1.43:1 ratio for immersive vertical compositions

### Rhythm
- Cross-cutting between parallel timelines; tempo builds with score
- Silent moments punctuated by overwhelming sound design
- Shot lengths vary: 10-second landscape holds → 1-frame action cuts

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
大画幅IMAX质感：65mm有机颗粒，自然光极限使用，角色在广阔环境中渺小。[天空/建筑几何]主导构图，水平线占据[上下2/3]。正交移动，极简轴向。无补光。
```

### Best For
- Sci-fi spectacle, war epics, historical dramas, biographical films, large-scale action

## 7. Mihai Malaimare Jr. — Classic Elegance

**Films:** The Master, Tár, Licorice Pizza, Youth Without Youth

### Core Philosophy
- 65mm film for creamy, warm, large-format beauty
- Classical Hollywood framing elevated with modern texture
- Soft, motivated light with rich shadow gradation
- Long takes with masterful blocking — actors move through the frame as camera holds
- Lens choice is deliberate and expressive — each focal length has emotional meaning

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Classic two-shots, masters with deep staging, occasional extreme close-up for punctuation
- **Movement:** Slow, deliberate dolly and tracking shots; camera rarely handheld
- **Lens preference:** 40mm, 50mm, 75mm on 65mm format

### Composition
- Balanced, classical composition with modern sensibility
- Actors blocked through depth — foreground/background simultaneously active
- Architecture and interior design complement character

### Rhythm
- Patient, observant — lets scenes breathe
- Average shot length 6-10 seconds; never rushes
- Emotional shifts are subtle, cumulative

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
65mm古典质感：奶油般温暖细腻的有机颗粒，受控柔光渐变至深邃阴影。经典构图搭配现代纹理，角色在画面纵深中调度。缓慢有意的移动镜头。
```

### Best For
- Character dramas, period pieces, prestige biopics, literary adaptations

## 8. Chung-hoon Chung — Neon Expressionist

**Films:** Oldboy, The Handmaiden, Last Night in Soho, It

### Core Philosophy
- Extreme color saturation and controlled artificial lighting
- Deep, rich blacks as negative space
- Neon practicals as both light source and color palette
- Precise, geometric framing with unexpected angles
- Anamorphic lensing for horizontal grandeur

### Shot Language
- **Framing:** Bold symmetry broken by asymmetric subject placement; tight, claustrophobic interiors
- **Movement:** Controlled dolly tracks, 90-degree angle changes, sudden crash zooms for punctuation
- **Lens preference:** 35mm, 50mm, 85mm anamorphic

### Composition
- Two-color complementary palettes (teal/orange, red/green, purple/yellow)
- Corridor shots with vanishing-point perspective
- Ceiling and floor visible simultaneously (ultra-wide or tilted)

### Rhythm
- Slow build → explosive release; patience then violence
- Sound design mirrors visual rhythm — silence to crescendo
- Shot lengths: 6-10 second patient holds punctuated by rapid 1-second cuts

### Lighting Formula (Chinese prompt)
```
霓虹表现主义：[互补色对比]饱和色彩，极暗背景中[霓虹灯/彩色灯泡]既是光源也是调色板。纵深走廊消失点透视。精确对称构图中角色位置不对称。变形宽银幕。
```

### Best For
- Horror, psychological thriller, Asian extreme cinema, gothic romance, stylish genre films

## Style Override Protocol

When the user specifies a director/DP reference:
1. Use that style's lighting formula, lens preferences, and movement vocabulary
2. Adapt the composition rules to the script's spatial constraints
3. Match the rhythm profile to the scene's emotional arc
4. Include a style declaration at the top of the prompt block: `【风格参考：Deakins/Lubezki/Fincher...】`

When no style is specified:
1. Consult the Style Selection Matrix above
2. Select the primary style for the scene's dominant emotion
3. If the scene shifts emotional register mid-scene, note the transition and adjust lens/lighting/movement accordingly
4. Include the rationale in a brief comment: `# 风格选择理由：[emotion] → [style]`

## Multi-Style Scenes

For scenes that shift emotional register (e.g., calm → violent reveal):
- Use style A for the first shot group, style B for the second
- Bridge with a transitional shot (slow push-in that shifts lighting from natural to artificial)
- Note the transition explicitly in the prompt: `# 风格过渡：Deakins → Richardson`
