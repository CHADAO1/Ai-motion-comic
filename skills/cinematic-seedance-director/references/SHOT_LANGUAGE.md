# Shot Language & Composition Reference

> Cinematographic toolkit for constructing world-class shot language and visual composition.
> Every Seedance prompt must reference concrete shot vocabulary — generic terms produce generic output.

---

## 1. Shot Scale Taxonomy

| Chinese Term | English Term        | Frame Coverage                        | Emotional Function                              |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 大远景        | Extreme Wide Shot   | Subject is a speck in vast environment | Establish scale, isolation, epic scope          |
| 远景          | Wide Shot (WS)      | Full body + significant environment   | Establish geography, character in context        |
| 全景          | Full Shot           | Full body head to toe                 | Physical performance, blocking, spatial relation|
| 中远景        | Medium Wide         | Knees to top of head                  | Social distance, group dynamics                  |
| 中景          | Medium Shot (MS)    | Waist to top of head                  | Conversation, professional interaction            |
| 中近景        | Medium Close-up    | Chest to top of head                  | Intimacy, emotional access, dialogue intensity    |
| 近景          | Close-up (CU)      | Head and shoulders                    | Emotional penetration, micro-expression reading   |
| 特写          | Close-up (tight)   | Face fills 70%+ of frame              | Internal thought, suppressed emotion, revelation  |
| 大特写        | Extreme Close-up   | Eyes, mouth, hands, object detail    | Critical detail, obsessive focus, visceral impact  |

### Scale Progression Rules
- **Normal conversation:** MS → MCU → CU as emotional intensity rises
- **Action sequence:** EWS → WS → MS → MCU (rapid compression) → WS (release)
- **Revelation/shock:** WS or MS → ECU on face (sudden scale jump)
- **Building dread:** WS with slowly shrinking negative space → CU without cutting (slow push)
- **Emotional collapse:** CU → ECU → detail insert (hands trembling, eyes watering)

## 2. Camera Movement Vocabulary

### Static
| Term (CN/EN)         | Description                                     | When to Use                                    |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 固定镜头 (Locked-off)| Camera is completely motionless on tripod       | Symmetry, architectural framing, Fincher-style control, formal stillness |
| 缓推 (Slow Push-in)  | Imperceptible forward dolly, 0.5-1mm/sec feel   | Building intimacy, slow revelation, internal pressure, time dilating |
| 缓拉 (Slow Pull-out)| Imperceptible backward dolly                     | Growing isolation, release of tension, expanding context |

### Dynamic
| Term (CN/EN)         | Description                                     | When to Use                                    |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 环绕 (Orbit)         | Camera circles subject 90-360 degrees           | Character study, spatial immersion, Lubezki-style intimacy |
| 摇移 (Pan)           | Horizontal rotation from fixed position         | Revealing space, following action, scanning environment |
| 俯仰 (Tilt)          | Vertical rotation from fixed position           | Revealing height, scanning a body, vertical reveal |
| 跟踪 (Tracking)      | Camera moves alongside/behind/in front of subject| Following action, walking-and-talking, immersion |
| 升降 (Crane/Jib)     | Camera moves vertically via crane/jib/Steadicam-rig| Revealing scope, power dynamics, emotional ascension/descent |
| 手持 (Handheld)      | Human-held, breathing movement                   | Tension, realism, anxiety, combat, chaos        |
| 呼吸手持 (Breathing Handheld) | Softly swaying handheld, micro-movements | Intimacy without chaos, Doyle-style closeness, living-room realism |
| 抖动手持 (Shaky Handheld)  | Aggressive, unstable handheld                    | Combat, panic, terror, disorientation           |
| 甩摇 (Whip Pan)      | Fast pan creating motion blur, used as a cut     | Energy, transitions, Tarantino/Richardson energy |
| 撞推 (Crash Zoom)   | Rapid push-in, intentional                      | Sudden punctuation, comedic or violent emphasis  |

### Movement-to-Emotion Mapping
| Character Emotion     | Camera Movement                                | Rationale                                    |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Calm / contemplative   | Locked-off or slow push-in (0.5mm/sec)         | Stillness mirrors inner peace                |
| Building tension      | Slow push-in accelerating to 2mm/sec           | Pressure compresses space around character    |
| Panic / terror        | Shaky handheld, whip pans, crash zooms        | Camera loses control as character does        |
| Revelation / shock    | Static → sudden slow pull-out (context)        | Freeze then expand: the world shifts          |
| Anger / rage          | Aggressive handheld, fast tracking             | Camera becomes a weapon, tracking fury       |
| Sadness / grief       | Locked-off or slow pull-out, breath handheld  | Stillness honors the weight of emotion        |
| Joy / freedom         | Crane up, orbit, tracking forward             | Camera liberates, moves with the character   |
| Intoxication / altered state | Dutch tilt, step-printed slow-motion, drifting handheld | Reality bends, time distorts |
| Surveillance / being watched | Static, long-lens, partially occluded | Voyeuristic, threatening, distant            |

## 3. Lens Selection Guide

| Focal Length | CN Term   | Emotional Function                              | Depth of Field | When to Choose                           |
|--------------|-----------|-------------------------------------------------|----------------|------------------------------------------|
| 14-24mm      | 超广角     | Distortion, vastness, unease, architectural awe | Deep (F8+)     | Epic scale, interior distortion, action impact |
| 28-35mm      | 广角       | Environmental storytelling, character in context| Moderate       | Deakins environmental, Lubezki intimacy-at-distance |
| 40-50mm      | 标准       | Natural perspective, documentary neutrality     | Moderate-Deep   | Dialogue, observational realism, Fincher grid |
| 50mm F1.4    | 标准浅景深  | Intimate close-up, creamy bokeh, emotional isolation | Shallow | Close-up dialogue, romantic isolation, Doyle chromatic |
| 75-100mm      | 中长焦     | Compressed depth, flattering portraits           | Shallow         | Beauty close-ups, emotional CU, compressed background |
| 100-150mm    | 长焦       | Extreme compression, surveillance, sports, nature  | Very Shallow    | Voyeuristic distance, telephoto compression, nature doc |
| 85mm Macro   | 微距       | Skin texture, object detail, tactile immersion   | Razor-thin      | Insert shots, extreme detail, tactile horror/beauty |

### Aperture as Emotional Tool
- **F1.2 - F1.8:** Dreamy isolation, romantic blur, creamy bokeh → use for intimacy, memory, dream sequences
- **F2.0 - F2.8:** Shallow but legible → use for dialogue close-ups, emotional penetration
- **F4.0 - F5.6:** Moderate depth → use for dialogue masters, group shots, environmental portrait
- **F8.0 - F11:** Deep focus → use for landscape, architecture, Fincher-style clinical clarity, everything-in-focus dread

## 4. Composition Principles

### 4.1 Rule of Thirds (三分法)
- Place subject's eyes on upper third horizontal line
- Place subject on vertical third line (left or right)
- Negative space opposite the subject's gaze direction
- **Best for:** Standard dialogue, establishing shots, naturalistic composition

### 4.2 Golden Ratio / Fibonacci Spiral (黄金螺旋)
- Subject placed at the spiral's tightest curl
- Leading lines curve toward the focal point
- **Best for:** Organic, flowing compositions; nature; Lubezki-style imagery

### 4.3 Symmetrical / Center Frame (对称构图)
- Subject exactly centered, environment mirrors left-right
- Creates formality, unease, power, or Kubrickian dread
- **Best for:** Fincher precision, Kubrick geometry, institutional spaces, corridors

### 4.4 Leading Lines (引导线构图)
- Architecture, roads, corridors, fences, shadows draw eye to subject
- Creates depth, journey, narrative pull
- **Best for:** Corridors, roads, urban environments, Chung-style depth

### 4.5 Frame Within a Frame (框中框)
- Doorways, windows, mirrors, arches create secondary frame around subject
- Implies surveillance, enclosure, separation
- **Best for:** Voyeurism, institutional spaces, Wong Kar-wai peeping intimacy

### 4.6 Negative Space (负空间)
- Subject occupies 10-30% of frame; rest is empty/environment
- Emptiness carries emotional weight: loneliness, freedom, threat of the unknown
- **Best for:** Deakins existential wide shots, Hoytema scale, isolation themes

### 4.7 Foreground Occlusion (前景遮挡)
- Objects between camera and subject partially block the view
- Creates intimacy-through-spying, layers of separation, documentary realism
- **Best for:** Doyle/Wong Kar-wai peeping, Lubezki natural layers, thriller surveillance

### 4.8 Dutch Angle (荷兰角/倾斜构图)
- Camera tilted 5-25 degrees from level
- Signals disorientation, unease, altered mental state, threat
- **Best for:** Horror, intoxication, psychological breakdown, Richardson aggression

### 4.9 Depth Staging (纵深调度)
- Multiple planes of action: foreground + midground + background
- All three planes carry narrative information
- **Best for:** Master shots, Malaimare classical, Fincher deep-focus dread

### 4.10 Symmetry Break (对称破坏)
- Symmetrical environment with subject placed asymmetrically
- Tension between order (environment) and chaos (character)
- **Best for:** Fincher institutional spaces, psychological disorder in structured settings

## 5. Angle of View

| Angle           | CN Term    | Emotional Function                              | When to Use                           |
|-----------------|------------|-------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Bird's Eye / Top| 俯拍/鸟瞰   | God-like perspective, vulnerability, pattern view| Death/transition, choreography, design overhead |
| High Angle      | 仰角拍摄（上俯） | Character diminished, vulnerable, observed     | Subject is weak, trapped, being watched|
| Eye Level      | 平视        | Equality, documentary truth, natural            | Standard dialogue, realism            |
| Low Angle       | 俯角拍摄（下仰）| Character empowered, mythic, threatening       | Heroic shots, villain power, Richardson angle |
| Dutch/Tilted   | 倾斜角      | Disorientation, unease, altered reality         | Psychological distress, horror        |

## 6. Shot Transition Vocabulary

| Transition (CN/EN)     | Description                                           | When to Use                              |
|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 硬切 (Hard Cut)        | Direct cut to next shot                               | Standard; action; rapid scene coverage   |
| 动作匹配切 (Match Cut on Action) | Cut during a continuous motion (door opening, turning) | Invisible transitions between angles     |
| 视线匹配切 (Eyeline Match) | Cut to what character is looking at                   | POV reveal, subject identification        |
| 甩摇转场 (Whip Pan Transition) | Fast pan creates blur, landing on new scene           | Energy, temporal jump, Tarantino energy  |
| 淡入/淡出 (Fade In/Out)  | Gradual light/dark transition                         | Scene ending, chapter break, time passage|
| 叠化 (Dissolve)         | One image fades as another emerges                    | Memory, time passage, emotional continuity|
| 长镜头 (Long Take / Oner) | No cut; entire scene in single take                   | Immersion, real-time, Lubezki/Children of Men |

## 7. Spatial Depth Categories

| Depth Type         | Description                                    | Emotional Function                       |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 浅景深 (Shallow)    | F1.2-F2.8; background dissolves to bokeh      | Isolation, dream, romantic abstraction   |
| 中景深 (Moderate)   | F2.8-F5.6; background legible but soft         | Dialogue intimacy with context           |
| 深景深 (Deep)       | F8-F16; everything razor-sharp                 | Environmental storytelling, dread, surveillance |
| 分层景深 (Layered)  | Multiple focus planes at different distances   | Complex narrative staging, master shots  |

## 8. Composition in Seedance Prompts

When writing Seedance prompts, composition is expressed through:

### 8.1 Framing Declaration
```
【构图】三分法，角色位于右侧三分线，目光朝向左侧负空间。背景为[具体环境描述]。
```

### 8.2 Depth & Focus Declaration
```
【景深】浅景深，F1.8，50mm镜头，角色面部锐利，背景融化至奶油散景。
```

### 8.3 Movement Declaration
```
【镜头运动】缓推，从全景以0.5mm/秒的速度逼近至特写，角色保持静止但呼吸可见。
```

### 8.4 Angle Declaration
```
【拍摄角度】微仰角（5度），角色在环境中略显压迫感，天花板可见。
```

### 8.5 Multi-Shot Sequence
```
【镜头1】大远景，固定，15秒。环境全景建立空间。
【镜头2】中景，缓推至中近景，10秒。引入角色。
【镜头3】近景，手持呼吸感，5秒。情绪穿透。
```

Each shot in a multi-shot prompt gets its own 【镜头N】 block with:
- 机位 (camera position)
- 焦距 (lens)
- 景深 (depth of field)
- 运动 (movement)
- 构图 (composition)
- 拍摄角度 (angle)
